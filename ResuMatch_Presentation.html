ResuMatch

AI-Powered Job Matching for Students and Employers

ResuMatch connects job seekers to the best-fit jobs using advanced resume and job description matching with similarity scoring.

Problem Statement

  • Job seekers struggle to find opportunities that match their skills and resume details.
  • Employers receive many applications but need the best candidates quickly.
  • Traditional job search platforms do not provide accurate skill-to-job alignment.

ResuMatch solves this with an intelligent matching engine that focuses on relevance, not just keywords.

ResuMatch Solution

  • AI-backed resume analysis and employer requirement matching.
  • Similarity scoring using TF-IDF and cosine similarity.
  • Fast candidate ranking and job recommendation.

The system evaluates job descriptions and resumes to compute a compatibility percentage for every candidate-job pair.

Core Features

  • Resume upload and parsing
  • Employer job posting and image support
  • Role-based access for students, employers, and admins
  • Real-time matching and job recommendations

How It Works

  1. Users upload resumes and enter skills, education, experience.
  2. Employers create job posts with required skills and descriptions.
  3. The engine computes similarity scores between resumes and job descriptions.
  4. Candidates are ranked and matched to jobs with relevance-based scores.

Matching is based on meaningful skill and keyword importance, not simple text matching.

Technology Stack

  • Backend: Node.js, Express, MongoDB
  • Frontend: Vite, React
  • Matching logic: TF-IDF and cosine similarity
  • Security: role-based auth, admin and employer controls

Modular architecture supports scalable data flow and user-friendly interfaces.

Why ResuMatch Stands Out

  • Improves hiring accuracy for employers
  • Helps students present the right job matches
  • Reduces manual application review time
  • Supports smarter, faster placement decisions

Future Enhancements

  • Automated resume suggestions and formatting tips
  • AI interview preparation and score explanations
  • More advanced analytics for employers and students
  • Multi-language and multi-country resume matching

Thank You!

ResuMatch is designed to make candidate-job matching smarter, faster, and more reliable.
